Laying the roots of heavy fabrication and industrial manufacturing in New Delhi.

1972 – 1989

Establishment of WINSTEEL

Winsteel Engineering Works Pvt. Ltd. was formally established by visionary structural engineers with a dedicated mission to deliver robust, precision heavy steel equipment and custom fabrication solutions.

Inception in New Delhi

Manufacturing of Chemical Equipments

Expanded heavy manufacturing capabilities into specialized chemical processing vessels, industrial reactors, and high-pressure storage tanks requiring certified plate bending and weld integrity.

CHAPTER 02: SEGMENTAL MOULDS & RMC EXPANSION

Entering national construction equipment and pioneering India's first bridge segmental moulds.

1990 – 1999
1990

Manufacturing of Construction Equipments

Strategically pivoted towards the civil construction boom, engineering heavy shuttering units, bridge falsework, and custom structural steel supports for national highway corridors.

Civil Infrastructure Focus
1996

Started with manufacturing RMC batching equipment plant

Initiated the full-scale turnkey manufacturing of Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) batching plants, silos, and automated concrete mixing structures to accelerate rapid site execution.

Batching Plant Production
1998

Shifting Expansion from Old premises to current premises.

Accommodating a massive surge in national infrastructure demand for heavy bridge girders, Winsteel shifted its operations from the old facility to sprawling modern industrial premises equipped with high-tonnage overhead EOT crane bays and heavy fabrication lines.

Major Industrial Capacity Upgrade
1998

Manufacturing of first long line mould for segmental casting in India

Engineered and manufactured India's very first Long Line Mould for precast segmental bridge casting, revolutionizing metro and highway viaduct erection accuracy nationwide.

🇮🇳 Historic Indian First
1999

Manufacturing of first short line moulds for segmental casting

Following our long-line triumph, we engineered India's first Short Line Mould systems, enabling complex 3D curved precast segments with sub-millimeter hydraulic alignment.

🇮🇳 National Segmental Pioneer

CHAPTER 03: LAUNCHING GANTRIES & UNIT EXPANSION

Achieving self-reliance via import substitution and scaling manufacturing footprint.

2000 – 2010
2003

Manufacture of first accropod mould for breakwater system

Successfully fabricated India's first complex 3D steel Accropod moulds used for casting giant interlocking concrete armor units protecting coastal harbors and marine breakwaters.

Marine & Coastal Engineering
2004

Started manufacturing of rock grapples being import substitution

Pioneered the indigenous manufacturing of heavy-duty hydraulic Rock Grapples for port and quarry operations, successfully eliminating reliance on foreign European imports.

🏆 Proud Import Substitution
2004 • FLAGSHIP MILESTONE

Started manufacturing of LGs (Launching Gantries)

Commenced the complete turnkey design, heavy structural fabrication, and site commissioning of high-capacity overhead and under-slung **Launching Gantries (LGs)**. This milestone marked Winsteel's emergence as an indispensable partner for rapid span-by-span viaduct launching across India's biggest metro and highway corridors.

Heavy Launching Equipment Mastery
2007

Adding manufacturing Unit 2

Commissioned our second heavy fabrication plant (**Unit 2**) equipped with heavy CNC profile shearing and plate bending machines to support surging order volumes.

Multi-Plant Expansion
2009

Adding manufacturing Unit 3

Further expanded our industrial infrastructure by establishing **Unit 3**, dedicating covered crane bays exclusively to heavy girder fitment and automated SAW welding lines.

3rd Manufacturing Facility

CHAPTER 04: GLOBAL COLLAB, MEGA-UNITS & INDIA'S FIRSTS

International partnership with Alpi Sea Ltd., Units 4-5, and record-breaking national span moulds.

2017 – 2023
2017

Adding manufacturing Unit 4

Commissioned **Unit 4**, adding massive covered bays with 25 Ton EOT cranes to manufacture heavy form travellers and mega-gantry girder boxes under strict ISO quality control.

4th Industrial Complex
2018

Collaborated with technical partner with Alpisea (Alpi Sea Ltd.)

Signed a prestigious international technical partnership with **Alpi Sea Ltd.**, combining European engineering innovation with Winsteel's manufacturing mastery for global bridge systems.

🌐 World-Class Technical Collab
2020

Adding manufacturing Unit 5

Established **Unit 5**, bringing Winsteel's combined manufacturing infrastructure across 5 high-tech premises to an astounding 324,000 Sft Total Plot Area (`30,000 Sq mtr`) and 237,000 Sft Covered Bay capacity (`22,000 Sq mtr`) supported by 45+ EOT cranes.

5 High-Tech Premises Complete
2021 • NATIONAL BENCHMARK

First full span mould of India

Designed, fabricated, and delivered **India's First Full Span Mould System** for high-speed rail and heavy metro box girders, establishing a new national benchmark in rapid casting.

👑 National Record First
2023 • RECORD BREAKING

Longest U-Girder mould

Manufactured and commissioned **India's Longest U-Girder Mould** (`2023`), delivering flawless structural alignment and zero-deflection casting for mega urban metro corridors.
