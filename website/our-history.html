COMPANY HISTORY

Over five decades of pioneering engineering leadership, custom bridge equipment manufacturing, and unwavering structural safety.

52 YEARS OF EXCELLENCE
TIMELINE

Trace the milestone journey of Winsteel Engineering Works Pvt. Ltd. from its establishment in 1972 to becoming an international infrastructure powerhouse.

1972
Inception & Foundation

Establishment of WINSTEEL

Winsteel Engineering Works Pvt. Ltd. was established in New Delhi by visionary structural engineers with a mission to deliver precision custom-made heavy steel equipment for India's growing industrial sector.

1985
Heavy Manufacturing Expansion

Hydraulic & EOT Crane Assembly Bays

Commissioned specialized heavy hydraulic forming bays and installed our first heavy EOT cranes to manufacture customized bridge falsework, shuttering units, and structural steel girders.

1998
Landmark Infrastructure

National Highway & Flyover Leadership

Emerged as a primary equipment supplier for major Indian national highway corridors, river crossings, and urban elevated metro viaducts, delivering zero-failure structural launching systems.

2010
Global Technology Collab

Technical Collaboration with Alpi Sea Ltd.

Signed a prestigious long-term international technical collaboration with Alpi Sea Ltd. to design and engineer world-class Form Travellers, Beam Launchers, and Movable Scaffolding Systems (MSS).

2018
Robotic Automation Complex

Sprawling 324,000 Sft Modern Manufacturing

Consolidated and expanded manufacturing operations across 5 sprawling premises with automated CNC precision plasma cutting, robotic welding bays, and 45+ EOT cranes supporting 18,000 MT annual capacity.

2024+
Next-Gen Smart Equipment

Smart Bridge Launching & Global Turnkey Excellence

Continuously innovating with IoT-monitored hydraulic launching gantries, high-speed rail formworks, and custom offshore infrastructure solutions across India and international export markets.
