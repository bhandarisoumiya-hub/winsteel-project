ABOUT US

WINSTEEL ENGINEERING WORKS PVT. LTD. established in the Year 1972, has acquired a high reputation and earned experience in Manufacturing Custom made Equipments.

WINSTEEL is a Pioneer and leading Formwork and Falsework Manufacturer in India. We have a rich experience of nearly FIVE Decades. We provide Innovative, Safe and Smart Solutions to Construction Industry.

WINSTEEL is a leading Bridge Construction Equipment and Special Equipment manufacturer. Our Involvement is from the Inception to the Completion of the Project. We provide the complete Services right from understanding the project and client's requirement, Design, Manufacturing, Installation and Commissioning of Equipments, Site Support, Staff training.

WINSTEEL has expertise in Design, Manufacturing, Supply & Commissioning of Bridge Construction Equipments such as Custom made Formwork & Falsework Systems, Launching Gantries, Accropode Moulds for Breakwater System, Rock Grapples and RMC Batching Plant Equipments such as Cement Silos, Flyash Silos, Aggregate Bins, Aggregate Conveyors and so on.

WINSTEEL to add more value to the products and increase the range of products have made a Technical Collaboration with the International Company – M/S ALPI South East Asia Co. Ltd http://www.alpisea.com. With this Collaboration, we have increased our product ranges from Formworks to Launching Gantry, Form Travellers, Beam Launchers, Movable Scaffolding Systems etc and also added the services like Designing complex moulds, Formworks, Design of Casting Yard Layout and so on. With this we can provide a Value Engineering Solutions for various Bridge Construction Equipments.

WINSTEEL firmly believes in policy of augmenting the capability and resources in the organization for upgrading the technology and meeting the evolving demands of the Valued Customers.

Our Corporate philosophy is to keep pace with the magnitude of technological change, timely delivery of high quality products – that our clients shall vouch for. It has been our immense concern & endeavour to blend & synergise these facets in executing all our projects.
